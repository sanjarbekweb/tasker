Core Capabilities

Engineered for Academic Mastery

Scroll through the architecture that keeps your study rhythm unbroken across any device, with or without Wi-Fi.

1. Natural Language Parser

Type `"HW 3 tomorrow 5pm p1 #math"` — our deterministic tokenizer extracts dates, times, priority levels, and course tags instantly without sending personal prompts to remote AI servers.

2. Timestamp-Driven Focus Engine

Survives OS backgrounding, low-power suspension, and system reboots. Reconstructs exact remaining time from wall-clock timestamps (`target_at - now()`) and delivers local notifications on schedule.

3. Free-Gap Collision Detection

Parses `RRULE` recurring university schedules, maps class boundaries, and highlights open study intervals so you always know your next available focus window.

4. Local SQLite & Zero-Cloud Risk

OP-SQLite + Drizzle ORM ensures 0ms read/write latency. Optional SQLCipher hardware-backed encryption with SecureStore key isolation and lossless JSON backup portability.
